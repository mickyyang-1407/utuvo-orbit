# Launch copy / 發布文案

## Short description

A native macOS menu-bar utility that combines power, network and audio into one compact glyph, with quick controls and display preferences in one panel. MIT open source.

## 一句話

把 Mac 的電量／CPU、網路與音量，收進一顆選單列圖示。原生介面、同頁控制，MIT 開源。

## 繁中發布貼文

我做了一個想每天留在 Mac 選單列上的小工具：**UTUVO Orbit**。

筆電看電量，桌機看 CPU；中間是網路，下面的圓點是音量。平常只有一顆小圖示，點開就能換音訊輸出、調音量、保持喚醒，還能看流量和周邊電量。

我想讓它簡單到不需要翻設定：主題、百分比、合併或傳統樣式，全部放在同一個面板。SwiftUI／AppKit 原生製作，支援 Liquid Glass，資料留在本機。介面支援英文與繁體中文，底部就能切換。

現在把它用 MIT 授權開源，也準備了 Apple Silicon 安裝包。歡迎拿去改、回報問題，或一起把這顆小圖示做好。

下載與原始碼：https://github.com/mickyyang-1407/utuvo-orbit

安裝包目前採 ad-hoc 簽署、尚未 Apple 公證；請先看安裝說明。截圖為示範資料。

## English launch post

Meet **UTUVO Orbit** — your Mac, in one small orbit.

Battery on a MacBook. CPU on a desktop. Network in the center. Audio in the dots. One compact menu-bar glyph, with volume, output selection, timed keep-awake and live details a click away.

Switch between English and Traditional Chinese instantly from the footer. Display preferences stay in the same panel. Native SwiftUI and AppKit, Liquid Glass on supported macOS, no account or cloud service.

MIT source and Apple Silicon downloads are ready. Contributions and focused bug reports are welcome.

Source and downloads: https://github.com/mickyyang-1407/utuvo-orbit. Initial binaries are ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized; see the installation guide. Screenshots use demo data.
