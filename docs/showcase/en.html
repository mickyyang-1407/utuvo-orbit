Small in your menu bar.
Together in one panel.

Battery on a MacBook. CPU on a desktop. Open Orbit to change volume, choose an output, keep your Mac awake or change its look. Display preferences stay right below your status details.

01 / NETWORK

A clearer view of your connection.

See the active interface, local IP, upload and download activity, and recent trends. These are interface readings, not speed-test results.

02 / SYSTEM

Your Mac. And its companions.

CPU, memory and accessory batteries at a glance. The charging symbol appears only when macOS explicitly reports charging. Unknown readings stay unknown.
