# UTUVO Orbit 0.4.1 — In your language

Your Mac. In one small orbit.

![UTUVO Orbit — promotional artwork](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyyang-1407/utuvo-orbit/main/docs/assets/orbit-hero-gold.png)

The first public release of UTUVO Orbit: a free, MIT-licensed native Mac utility that brings power, network and audio into one compact menu-bar icon.

- Battery on a MacBook, CPU activity on a desktop.
- Volume, audio output selection and a timed keep-awake control.
- Network activity, system details and available accessory battery/charging readings.
- Combined or classic icons, inline appearance controls and Liquid Glass on supported macOS.

[English guide](https://github.com/mickyyang-1407/utuvo-orbit/blob/main/docs/USER-GUIDE.md) · [繁體中文說明](https://github.com/mickyyang-1407/utuvo-orbit/blob/main/docs/USER-GUIDE.zh-Hant.md)

## New in 0.4.1

- Full English and Traditional Chinese interface, including menus, status messages and accessibility labels.
- Use the globe menu at the bottom of the panel to choose System, English or 繁體中文. No restart needed.
- Refreshed screenshots and a complete English product showcase.

## 這次更新

App 完整支援英文與繁體中文。底部地球選單可選系統／English／繁體中文，立即切換並保存。維持四列整齊排版，設定同頁，不增加設定畫面。

這是 Orbit 首次公開發布：把電量／CPU、網路與音量放進一顆小圖示，點開即可切換音訊輸出、設定暫時保持喚醒、查看系統與周邊電量。免費使用，MIT 開源。
